*The Acts of the Day and the PEDLER Corpus*

corpus they govern, and the invitation the order exists to issue.

## The primitives

- **Act = executable intent** — a *walk* through transition structure. Not the ACT of ACT-R, which is
  rule firing in a production system.
- **Inclination = signed directional gradient over transition structure**, `I ∈ [−1, +1]`, defined on
  **edges**. Zero is directional neutrality, not cessation. Decay weakens magnitude and preserves
  sign. Lateral inhibition is incoming negative inclination, not low activation.
- **Natural Justice = a constraint or gate** on the transition from intent toward Act and execution.
- **Persistence ≠ permanence** — continuity of identity across admissible transitions.

## Two things worth knowing before you read

The 1996 tic-tac-toe system in Part III is a **working PEDLER** — 3⁹ states, cells in {−1,0,+1},
next transition recorded per state, KB fullness by entropy, knowledge merged by XOR. The lineage is
dated, not retrofitted.

The universality claim in Part IV is a **realization lattice, not a history**: `Mᵢ = Πᵢ(P)`. Finite
state machines, Turing machines, Markov processes, Boltzmann and Hopfield networks, ANNs, gradient
descent, reinforcement learning and attention are recovered as projections of PEDLER under specific
restrictions.
